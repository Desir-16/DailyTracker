# Daily Tracker Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 5 August 2026  
**Developer:** S M Shaif Mahamud Shemon  
**Contact:** [Daily Tracker support](SUPPORT.md)

## Summary

Daily Tracker is designed to work offline. The developer does not operate a server for the app and does not receive the habits, tasks, wellness entries, reminders, profile information, photos, or backups entered by users.

The app contains no advertising SDK, remote analytics SDK, account system, or behavioral tracking service.

## Information stored by the app

The app may store the following information on the user's device:

- Habits, targets, completion history, monthly focus, and analysis data
- Checklist tasks and completion history
- Mood and sleep entries
- Important dates and reminder settings
- Profile name, email label, profile photo, and cover photo when voluntarily added
- Theme, widget, tutorial, and other app settings

This information is used only to provide the app's requested features.

## Optional Google Drive or document-provider backup

Users may select a folder through Android's system file picker and create or restore a backup. If the selected provider is Google Drive, Android and Google handle access to that folder. The app does not receive the user's Google password and does not send the backup to a developer-operated server.

The backup may contain app data and user-selected profile images so that the app can be restored on another device. Backup creation and restoration are controlled from within the app.

## Calendar access

Calendar permission is optional. When granted, the app reads calendar events on the device to show relevant upcoming events in the important-dates widget. Calendar information is processed on the device and is not sent to the developer.

## Notifications and reminders

The app may request notification, exact-alarm, full-screen reminder, and restart-after-boot capabilities to deliver important-date reminders selected by the user. These capabilities do not transmit user information to the developer.

## Photos

Profile and cover photos are selected voluntarily by the user and stored for display inside the app. They may be included in a user-created backup. They are not uploaded to the developer.

## Data sharing

The developer does not sell or share app data. Android, Google Drive, or another document provider may process information when the user chooses that provider; their own privacy terms apply to those services.

## Data retention and deletion

App data remains on the device until the user removes it, clears the app's storage, restores a different backup, or uninstalls the app. Backups remain in the user-selected folder until the user deletes or replaces them.

## Children

The app is a general productivity tool and does not knowingly collect children's personal information through a developer-operated service.

## Security

No method of electronic storage is perfectly secure. Users should protect access to their device and any selected backup location.

## Changes

Material changes to this policy will be published with an updated effective date on the official Daily Tracker distribution page.

## Contact

Questions about this policy may be submitted through the official [Daily Tracker support page](SUPPORT.md).
